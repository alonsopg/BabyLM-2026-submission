\section{Limitations}

The current implementation uses heuristic token-level entity extraction rather than a full syntactic or coreference system. Error heavy hitters are keyed by target token string in the first implementation, so they do not distinguish different contexts for the same token. The approach may overemphasize repeated or difficult tokens without improving the linguistic generalizations measured by BabyLM.

The local training comparison and the available official BabyLM strict zero-shot evaluations are complete for the two main systems, but the local evaluation bundle did not include EWoK. We therefore do not report EWoK and cannot assess that part of the full BabyLM suite here. The diagnostic logs also show substantial fallback to random masking, so these results should not be interpreted as a strong test of a perfectly balanced entity/error/random target mixture. Finally, the official HHM gains are small and based on three seeds; paired significance tests do not establish reliable differences. They should be treated as suggestive diagnostics rather than a robust downstream improvement claim. CMS-Morph and Entity-Accuracy-Morph remain single-seed follow-ups, and Accuracy-Morph has only two matched seeds, so the follow-up results identify promising directions rather than final comparative performance. The component ablations reported here are short 20-step checks and should not be read as final ablation evidence.

The paper also does not attempt to match the strongest BabyLM systems. In particular, we do not use hybrid causal-masked objectives, architecture changes, optimizer changes, curated corpora, external lexical resources, or auxiliary representation modules. That restriction is scientifically useful for attribution, but it limits expected absolute performance. A stronger final system would likely combine the best controlled masking signal here, Accuracy-Morph, with a stronger BabyLM backbone such as a hybrid causal-masked model; that experiment would answer a different question from the one isolated in this paper.
